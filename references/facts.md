# Facts

The loose ends: things noticed in conversation that are not a defining trait of anyone, but would be a shame to lose. About the user, about the assistant, about the two of them together

Anything that finds no place in [user.md](user.md) or [assistant.md](assistant.md) lands here. When one of these stops being incidental and turns into a trait, it moves into a dossier and leaves this file — it does not live in two places

## Format

One line per fact, the date at the end in brackets, absolute rather than relative: "last week" is unreadable six months later

```
- The fact in one line, with no closing full stop (2026-01-31)
```
